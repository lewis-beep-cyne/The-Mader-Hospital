WELCOME TO THE MADER HOSPITAL

HISTORY

The Hospital was founded on 22nd March 2003, with its first branch in Kawangware, Nairobi County, Kenya.

MISSION

To provide efficient, sustainable, and better healthcare for all.

VISION

To be a leading centre in research, healthcare, and support giver in the region and beyond.

CORE VALUES

Integrity, Compassion, Excellence, and Innovation.

Accreditation

We are a SHA accredited institution, under the License Number 12345/KMPDC/2026, operating under the Social Health Insurance Act, 2023 and the Medical Practitioners and Dentists Act (Legal Notice No. 25 of 2000).
